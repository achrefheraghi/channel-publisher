# Privacy policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This policy covers Channel Publisher (the app), a private tool that publishes videos to its developer's own YouTube channels.

## What the app can access

The app asks Google for one permission: **Manage your YouTube account** (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube`). It uses it only on channels owned by the developer, to upload videos, set thumbnails, edit video and channel details, manage playlists and read the channels' own video statistics.

## How data is stored

- The Google sign-in token is saved only on the developer's own computer. It is never sent anywhere except to Google.
- The app has no server and no database. It sends nothing to the developer or to any third party.
- The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer YouTube or Google user data, and does not use it for advertising.

## Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs complies with the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services and is bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Deleting the local token file also ends the app's access.
