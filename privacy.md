# Privacy policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This policy covers Channel Publisher (the app), a private tool that publishes videos to its developer's own accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

## What the app can access

The app asks Google for one permission: **Manage your YouTube account** (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube`). It uses it only on channels owned by the developer, to upload videos, set thumbnails, edit video and channel details, manage playlists and read the channels' own video statistics.

## Facebook and Instagram (Meta)

The app asks Meta for permission to publish videos and Reels to one Facebook Page and one Instagram professional account, both owned by the developer, and to read basic publishing status and limits for them. It does not read private messages, friends lists or any other person's data. To publish an Instagram Reel, the video file may be placed briefly at a temporary public web address so Instagram can fetch it; it is deleted once Instagram has finished.

## TikTok

The app asks TikTok for the `video.upload` permission on the developer's own TikTok account. It uses it only to send finished videos to that account's inbox as drafts. The developer then reviews each draft, sets the AI-content label if needed, and publishes it by hand in the TikTok app. The app stores the account's TikTok open ID and its access tokens, and reads nothing else about the account or other users.

## How data is stored

- The sign-in tokens (Google, Meta, TikTok) are saved only on the developer's own computer. Each is sent only to the service that issued it.
- The app has no server and no database. It sends nothing to the developer or to any third party.
- The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer YouTube, Google, Meta or TikTok user data, and does not use it for advertising.

## Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs complies with the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services and is bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Meta access can be removed in Facebook and Instagram settings under Apps and Websites, and TikTok access in TikTok under Settings, Security, Manage app permissions. Deleting the local token files also ends the app's access. To ask for deletion of anything the app holds, email the address below; the only data held is the developer's own tokens.
